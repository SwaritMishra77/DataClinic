What this meansThese are controlled errors introduced into real datasets. A suspicious example may be genuinely difficult and correctly labelled. The ranking is a suggestion for review, never permission to delete data automatically.
What it does not meanThese experiments do not establish performance on naturally mislabelled production data or support clinical use. Results vary across the recorded splits; see the repository CSV files for every run.
